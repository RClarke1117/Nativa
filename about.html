Rooted in Newton

Nativa Coffee Bar is the next chapter from the family behind Patagonia Coffee House in Hackettstown — bringing the same passion for fresh pastry, ceremonial matcha, and unhurried hospitality to 216 Woodside Avenue.

The room is built for lingering: woven light, greenery, mismatched chairs, and tables meant for conversation. Mornings start with espresso and bakery cases; evenings open into fajitas, steaks, wine, and seasonal cocktails.

“A new name. A new story. The same family and the same passion behind it.”
